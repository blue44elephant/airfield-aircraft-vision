# AW-01D data and model attribution

RarePlanes Dataset, June 2020. J. Shermeyer, T. Hossler, A. Van Etten, D. Hogan, R. Lewis and D. Kim. In-Q-Tel / CosmiQ Works and AI.Reverie. Public real WorldView imagery and annotations; CC BY-SA 4.0. Native crops and overlays are derivatives and retain that license. Original pixels, sidecars and annotations remain unchanged. Source checksums, windows and overlay changes are recorded.

[Official dataset](https://www.iqt.org/library/the-rareplanes-dataset), [annotation guide](https://www.iqt.org/library/rareplanes-public-user-guide), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Independent NAIP windows: USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program; hosted/processed by Microsoft Planetary Computer and Esri. The [official USDA record](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/national-agriculture-imagery-program-naip-imagery) specifies Public Domain. The conflicting catalog machine license is retained in the contract evidence. This is aerial imagery, not satellite imagery; RGB selection/cropping does not alter physical resolution.

TorchVision detector architecture retains its [BSD license](https://github.com/pytorch/vision/blob/v0.25.0/LICENSE). Original AW-01/AW-01B/AW-01C author, dataset and pretrained-model notices are preserved. No unrestricted trained-weight redistribution or endorsement is asserted. Local images and checkpoints remain ignored except the explicitly attributed derivative examples.
